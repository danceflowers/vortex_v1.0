Sparse TensorCore: Ping-Pong BMem Architecture

Current BMem = 4 lines (16×16 FP9 = one m16n16k16 tile). For 2:4 / 1:4 sparsity, K dimension grows 2×/4×, requiring multiple B chunks.

Current: Dense m16n16k16 (sparse_none)

Key Design Questions

What we need to decide
  1. Where does ping-pong live? Inside BMem class, or in ComputePipeline (Stage3)?
  2. Who triggers B load? Does ComputePipeline request B loads mid-compute, or does OperandFetch pre-load all chunks?
  3. LMEM bandwidth: B loads come from LMEM. Does ping-pong loading share the same LmemReadReq port as Stage2a's B fetch?
  4. K-phase mapping: With 2/4 K rounds, how does mem_k_phase() change? Each round's 4 BMem lines map to one logical K-phase.
  5. Accum phase count: 2:4 = 2 accum phases × 2 rounds = 4 total? Or something different?
